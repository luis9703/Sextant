# Future pathways from a present signal

Adapt this scaffold to the request; these prompts are not mandatory fields or a fixed number of sections. Write in the user's language and remove template guidance from the final artifact.

Start with concrete future possibilities and why their differences matter. Mark fictional scenes as illustrations, and give the scope, information date, research limits, and actual review status compactly.

For each retained pathway, explain the change in people's lives, the transitions that could lead there, key human desires and responses, enabling conditions, feedback, and failure branches. Compare it to the closest existing account and state what is added or still unknown. Put source links near present-day factual claims.

Show the principal differences with a diagram or comparison when helpful. Paths can overlap; do not force probabilities or exact years onto them.

Finish with observations that could distinguish the paths, where those observations could come from, what is not currently measurable, and how conflicting evidence would change the view. Add actions only if relevant to the user's purpose. Keep unresolved questions visible.

Keep the process record separately: initial candidates, research findings, second-pass changes, citations and counterevidence, reviewer dispositions, and any forecast or update history. Save concise justifications rather than private chain-of-thought.
