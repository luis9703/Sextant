# Review without manufacturing certainty

A fresh reviewer receives the user question, scope and cutoff, neutral candidate summaries, assumptions, evidence and counterevidence, plus access to necessary sources. Exclude the generation history, author identity, rankings, and intended verdict. This removes one source of influence; it does not make a shared model or source statistically independent.

Use an isolated invocation or bounded subagent only when the host supports it and it is within the current authorization. Do not create a user-owned task, purchase tools, or contact others for this step. If unavailable, label the actual same-context review. Never claim an expert or independent review that did not occur.

Begin by identifying which possibility most expands the question and how it could be made more concrete. Then distinguish:

- A factual contradiction or a source that fails to support the premise.
- A missing transition, resource, actor response, cultural condition, or delay.
- A plausible alternative explanation or analogous case that fails differently.
- A speculative condition that can remain explicit without being called established.
- A repair that strengthens the candidate, or a failure that opens another path.

Challenge both unjustified certainty and unjustified rejection. Before discarding an unfamiliar future, name the established constraint it contradicts; lack of precedent or a missing adoption series alone is a gap, not a contradiction. Seek a useful conditional bridge or a different form without protecting ideas from genuine counterevidence. Review material premises and consequential inferences proportionally; do not demand historical proof for fictional details or a citation for every future consequence.

Record the challenge, its basis, the disposition, and remaining disagreement. Keep, revise, downgrade, drop, and unresolved are all valid. Use separate or mixed dispositions for separable issues. An unchanged probability can be justified. Newness is neither a reason to accept a claim nor a reason to reject it.

Separate supported corrections to current premises from proposed causal mechanisms. A cited product property can be accurate while the suggested consequence is wrong. For a claimed exclusion, bottleneck or mandatory transition, consider whether feasible alternatives defeat it before rewriting the future around it. Do not grant a repair authority because it came from a fresh reviewer. In ordinary prose, explain what is supported, what remains a proposal, and why it is used, partly used, rejected or retained as unresolved; no additional issue schema or second review gate is required.

If a material premise fails, keep the imaginative question open while judging the failed bridge. A justified alternative condition may preserve or transform the future; a contradiction may also end it. Develop a meaningful alternative through the workbench when it could change the answer. Do not make the scene more dramatic by inventing exclusivity, and do not add unlimited assumptions just to save it. A correct sourced fact need not be adopted together with an unsupported mechanism built on it.

Save the actual review in an appropriate form. Prefer a reviewer-produced issue summary when it preserves consequential objections, their basis, uncertainty and proposed creative questions. A full reply may be retained as an optional source. If the orchestrator compresses a reply, label it issue_summary and state the coverage limits; do not title the summary as a verbatim original. Compression alone is not unperformed review. Missing substantive coverage is a limit to declare, not a word-count failure.

Before requesting review, identify the candidate-changing questions and source boundaries. Ask for grounding and creative challenge proportionally, not a second exhaustive industry survey. New citations are research leads until checked. Verify new current facts only when they are used in a consequential premise or repair. Park unused peripheral leads explicitly as not_used; do not verify every recalled case merely because it appeared in the reply. A future mechanism can be examined conditionally without collecting adoption history that does not exist.

Register consequential objections using review-add, with stable ids, the saved review source, and affected targets. Material issues default to pathways and report; narrow targets when warranted. Group unused peripheral leads rather than manufacture an issue for every source. Representation, context and declared coverage are stored separately on the review receipt. A faithful summary can complete the performed review; a failed invocation or unperformed review remains partial. If no material issue was raised, record a concrete no-issues reason. The checker cannot discover objections omitted from this declaration.

After deciding actual repairs, update the pathway synthesis with a new version using revise, keeping its previous draft as history. If no change is warranted, explicitly confirm the current synthesis with a reason. Finish the reader-facing report, re-open both current files with line numbers, then write dispositions describing those saved files. Important accepted premise corrections must propagate into their dependent pathways as well as report prose. 00-current.md identifies the current versions, so an earlier draft is not the authoritative reviewed synthesis.

For every registered id, use a disposition marker, a following plain-language rationale and actual excerpts for the affected targets. English and Chinese markers are both supported. Canonical actions are revise, keep, drop, unresolved and not_used; Chinese aliases are 修改, 保留, 移除, 未决 and 不采用. For example (the text and line numbers are illustrative):

```markdown
[处置 F1: 修改]
The changed premise and its dependency have been revised in both files.
[路径摘录 F1](06-pathways-v2.md#L12)
> A corrected condition from the saved synthesis.
[报告摘录 F1](report.md#L15)
> A corrected consequence from the saved report.
[处置 L1: 不采用]
The peripheral lead is not used by a dependent claim.
```

English equivalents are [Disposition F1: revise], [Pathway excerpt F1] and [Report excerpt F1]. When issues are registered, every excerpt must explicitly name its registered id; no id is inferred from a preceding disposition. A location for F1 cannot cover F2. If no issues were registered, an unscoped Report excerpt or 报告摘录 can identify a consequential retained claim. Paths are relative to the disposition note, with spaces percent-encoded. Quotes are nonempty contiguous substrings of the actual line, including any Markdown characters. Do not paraphrase inside the quote.

Choose the shortest passage that demonstrates the repair, and explain its relevance once. Do not paste whole paragraphs merely to enlarge the audit record. A new reader-facing document may also serve as the revised synthesis, when it actually integrates the current pathways; bind that same new file for both roles and locate each affected role. This avoids repeating the creative work or splitting an identical reviewed account into two copies. Historical phase artifacts and the disposition note remain separate.

Revise, keep, drop and unresolved need excerpts for each registered affected target. Not_used needs a reason rather than an invented passage. A material correction affecting pathways cannot be completed with only a report excerpt or unchanged synthesis. One valid F1 location cannot substitute for a missing F2 disposition. Coverage and literal matching do not prove the repair is correct; inspect the original review and dependent claims for unregistered issues or insufficient fixes. Mixed and unresolved dispositions are valid, and proposed work cannot be described as implemented.

Check the final lead, affected transitions and observations for withdrawn premises and unresolved assumptions, including qualitative comparisons such as “more likely” or “easier”: name the comparison and the conditions that support it, or retain it as an unranked possibility. Retaining a former claim explicitly as a rejected idea or a scope limitation is different from continuing to use it as supporting evidence.

Finish current synthesis and report edits and refresh affected locators before recording reconciliation. Use `--report` as described in [execution.md](execution.md) to bind its saved bytes; the deliver receipt must include that file unchanged. The helper checks report binding and literal quote locations, not whether the chosen passage actually resolves an objection or whether uncited repairs were omitted. Without the helper, record the actual file/version checked and the unavailable integrity check. If checking cannot be finished, preserve the report and concrete limits as partial work; do not manufacture completion evidence.

Reviewer recollections are research leads, not evidence. Spend remaining verification resources on leads that can materially change a path; preserve the checked source and label unresolved leads unverified. Check reviewer-proposed causal repairs too: does a signal distinguish competing explanations, does an example support the stated scope, and does a condition really exclude alternatives? A replacement mechanism is a candidate to examine, not authority to recast all futures around it. Never add all recalled sources just to make the report look researched. Preserve useful detail in phase artifacts while keeping the reader-facing report coherent. The user's scope and the Skill's evidence rules outrank a reviewer's request for false precision or extra work. If a new context completes reconciliation, record the handoff and exactly what it received.

Deep review covers two jobs: grounding and creative challenge. Grounding checks numbers, branch-specific dependencies, evidence status and whether observations distinguish alternatives. Creative challenge checks whether one causal account dominates all candidates, current-product constraints suppressed a broader future, human desires are assumed fixed, or a worthwhile failure branch remains only a footnote. It can propose another explanation or repair without demanding novelty or optimism. The same fresh reviewer may do both; the orchestrator launches reviewers directly rather than asking generating subagents to create nested reviewers.

Check the developed future after its enabling assumptions hold, not only the list of prerequisites. Check whether choices differ for a reason across actors in a comparable situation and what flips those choices; for commercial questions, inspect demand, use, payment and value capture separately. When an important gap needs another creative pass, return a concrete question for the [development workbench](development.md). The generating agent opens and performs that work before reconciliation rather than marking a proposed future edit as implemented. During final reconciliation, check the developed consequences, comparison and temporal dependencies survived compression. Queue completion alone does not settle these questions.

If a review could not be performed, record it as partial with its concrete limit. If a same-context or limited-scope review was performed, declare that context and coverage with a reason rather than claiming isolation or a full-source audit. Unresolved facts remain local conditions; unused leads need not become mandatory research. Report integrity, execution completeness, declared review scope and semantic judgments separately. Useful conditional futures can be delivered with those limits visible.

For public-facing claims, distinguish package validation, a worked example, model review, expert evaluation, and prospective prediction performance. None substitutes for the others. BTF-2 motivates attention to blind spots and strategic conditions but explicitly limits causal attribution of individual reasoning factors: [paper, discussion and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.26106v1).

When recording an actual binary forecast, define its question, scope, forecast timestamp, event deadline, probability basis, resolution source and rule, and treatment of ambiguous or unavailable outcomes. A draft may remain unquantified. Never interpret an unresolved event as false. Preserve old forecasts; changing the question requires an explicit new version. Scenario narratives need not become scoreable predictions.

For a later update, read the previous case before searching, state the newly available evidence, and identify what changed and what did not. Do not rewrite history to suggest a conclusion was held earlier. A signpost should specify how observing its opposite would change the assessment.

Project mechanism notes are optional and user-authorized. Each reusable lesson needs its conditions, source cases, counterexamples, discriminating signals, and status: hypothesis, supported observation, contested, or unknown. Repeated model outputs are not independent support, and one correct forecast does not establish its mechanism. Retrieve relevant notes after initial divergence to limit fixation; keep held-out evaluation answers and later outcomes out of the case's information set.

Scheduling, automatic learning, and public publication are separate capabilities. Do not imply that a list of observations has scheduled a monitor or that a Markdown lesson has trained the model.
