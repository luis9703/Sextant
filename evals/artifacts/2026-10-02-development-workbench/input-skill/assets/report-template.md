# Future pathways from a present signal

Adapt this scaffold to the request; these prompts are not mandatory fields or a fixed number of sections. Write in the user's language and remove template guidance from the final artifact.

Start with concrete future possibilities that the usual framing makes easy to miss: what people can newly do or want, which unfamiliar use appears, and why the change matters. Explain the bridge from the seed through complementary changes and responses to the wider world. Keep consequential unresolved assumptions visible; do not present one candidate as the whole future. Mark fictional scenes as illustrations, and keep scope, research limits and process status compact.

The default depth is deep even for “imagine.” Identify the actual profile and any incomplete phases. Put the receipt checker result in a separate verification artifact so the recorded report is not altered afterward. State observed Token usage only when the host supplies it; otherwise state unknown. A target budget is not measured usage.

For each retained pathway, explain the change in people's lives, the transitions that could lead there, key human desires and responses, enabling conditions, feedback, and failure branches. Develop worthwhile alternatives opened by a failure or changed constraint as futures in their own right, with their conditions visible. Compare each pathway to the closest existing account and state what is added or still unknown. Put source links near present-day factual claims.

Bring the valuable development work into the reader's account: what happens after enabling conditions hold, how repeated responses change the world, and whose choices differ under which conditions. Explain what could reverse those choices. Commercial questions distinguish new activity from replacement, and need from direct use, payment and its recipient; other questions can compare nonmonetary choices. Keep the pathway's relative timing and prerequisite order visible through summarization. The work queue and its commands belong in the process record, not in the user's imagined world.

Carry uncertain source claims into dependent conclusions. Preserve separate conditions for each path and separate current-product form from broader future possibilities. Explain the basis and conditions of qualitative rankings, or leave possibilities unranked. Finish this report before the final disposition record, then re-open it to check actual repair passages, including numbers, units, causal claims and withdrawn assumptions. Record reconciliation against this saved file, and deliver it unchanged.

Show the principal differences with a diagram or comparison when helpful. Paths can overlap; do not force probabilities or exact years onto them.

Finish with observations that could distinguish the paths, where those observations could come from, what is not currently measurable, and how conflicting evidence would change the view. Add actions only if relevant to the user's purpose. Keep unresolved questions visible.

Keep the process record separately: initial candidates, research findings, second-pass changes, citations and counterevidence, reviewer dispositions, and any forecast or update history. Save concise justifications rather than private chain-of-thought.
