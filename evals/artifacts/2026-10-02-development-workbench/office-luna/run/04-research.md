# Research that challenged and expanded the candidates

Accessed 2026-10-02. Sources are primary/official where feasible. Search results were opened as source pages in web research; notes below summarize only the relevant sections.

## Corrections and material evidence

- **Correction to foothold F3:** the cited URL is an MDN reference, not W3C. Use the primary W3C HTML specification page instead: https://www.w3.org/TR/html/all/ . W3C says HTML is the Web's core markup language, originally designed for semantic description of documents and later adapted to other document and application types. This establishes broad expressiveness of HTML/web apps, not that HTML is what “everyone” uses or that it replaces editing/record systems.
- **Commercial demand remains visible in incumbent results, but is not a causal AI test.** Microsoft's FY2026 Form 10-K reports Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue +17%, seats +6%, Consumer cloud revenue +28%, Consumer subscribers +7%, with cloud revenue per user also contributing. Commercial subscription revenue includes multiple products/services (including Teams, SharePoint, security/compliance and Copilot); therefore it cannot be read as Office editor-only demand or as proof of future direction. FY2026 compared to FY2025. Primary filing: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm
- **Chinese vendor evidence points in the same near-term direction with caveat.** Kingsoft's 2025 annual report says office software and services revenue grew 16% to RMB 5,928.7m; it attributes WPS individual growth partly to paying subscribers and broader scenarios / WPS AI upgrades, WPS 365 growth partly to document-AI-collaboration integration and enterprise expansion, and software orders partly to government/enterprise orders. This is management's attribution, not causal independent measurement; company segment covers more than standalone WPS editor. Primary filing: https://ir.kingsoft.com/static-files/999c2fb6-9739-4c00-a3d7-ec00dc5430b6
- **HTML can be an application surface, but offline and file workflows reveal complements.** W3C describes an open platform for rich interactive experiences across devices and cites broad extensions (CSS, SVG, APIs). Google documents web access and offline editing of recent Docs/Sheets/Slides with setup, browser and storage conditions. It also documents Office-file editing, auto-save in original format, conversion, and compatibility warnings. This reveals hybrid paths, not an inherent winner: browser UI can coexist with durable Office formats and local caches. Sources: https://www.w3.org/standards/ ; https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6388102?hl=en-NZ ; https://support.google.com/docs/answer/9406611?hl=en-419
- **A file is a content contract as well as a visual wrapper.** Ecma's ECMA-376 defines OOXML vocabularies, document representation and packaging. Microsoft documents Word/Excel/PowerPoint formats and explicit coauthoring eligibility; HTML is listed as local-only for Office coauthoring in the described WOPI flow. Microsoft also notes interoperability depends on both format and application support. This is concrete evidence that “HTML presentation” and “spreadsheet/document system of record” are different layers today, while also identifying a design surface that can change. Sources: https://ecma-international.org/publications-and-standards/standards/ecma-376/ ; https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/cloud-storage-partner-program/plus/extensions ; https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office/open-xml/general/introduction-to-markup-compatibility
- **Trust/governance is real operational product territory, not just UI.** Microsoft documents audit-event collection and retention, eDiscovery, retention, security and permissions services around its cloud. This supports a possible value-capture layer around accountable work records. It is vendor documentation of features, not evidence that buyers will always pay incumbents or that AI-produced web objects cannot provide equivalents. Sources: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/compliance/assurance/assurance-microsoft-365-audit-log-collection ; https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/compliance/advanced-audit?view=o365-worldwide ; https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/compliance/assurance/assurance-data-immutability
- **Office AI is already being positioned as file-native agentic creation.** Microsoft describes Copilot agents that create Word, Excel and PowerPoint files stored in a tenant, with organizational-data access subject to licensing/admin controls. The existence and product description support convergence of agent and suite; claims about quality, use, economics and retention remain unverified. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/copilot/microsoft-365/wordexcelppt-agents

## What research changed or did not change

- The “HTML replaces Office” binary is too coarse: browser presentation, authoring surface, structured data model, executable calculation, identity/governance, collaboration and archival contract can split apart.
- Initial candidates about invisible agents and governed execution are strengthened as live product strategies and complements, not settled outcomes.
- The `refuse files` and `living objects` paths remain possible: official product support and format standards describe current constraints, not hard limits on new products or norms.
- The “local/private execution” path remains conditional; available sources establish current offline provisions but do not quantify demand in disconnected or sovereignty-sensitive users.
- Search did not establish a credible population denominator for “everyone uses HTML”, global office-suite user/revenue split, willingness to pay by segment, AI impact attribution, or comparative conversion fidelity. Do not use such numbers as premises.
