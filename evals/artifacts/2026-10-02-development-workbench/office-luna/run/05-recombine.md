# Recombination after research

Research did not verify HTML's universal adoption. It did show active browser, file, cloud and agent paths coexist, while vendors report present growth across bundles. The problem therefore changes from “will Office survive HTML?” to “which layer of knowledge work is carried by which interface and who owns the durable record?”

## Candidates and material changes

1. **Agent as author; suite as substrate (refined from candidate 1/3).** Previously: people might stop opening apps but suites retain storage/governance. Changed dependency: agents already create named Office artifacts within tenant controls, while commercial products bundle identity, content and apps. Consequence: the app may become a review/exception console entered only when ambiguity, layout, formulas or approval matter. If agents can safely change business objects and expose provenance, keyboard-level authoring shrinks; if errors are hard to audit, humans return to direct editing. The important new consequence is that revenue could persist through tenant control while visible human app time falls.
2. **HTML as lens; typed source as work object (refined from candidate 2).** Earlier concept was a structured source with audience-specific HTML. Research shows current interoperability and coauthoring are mediated by both format and application support. Revised mechanism: HTML views make a work object linkable, interactive and audience-tuned, while the source model holds formulas, permissions, change history, citations and editable structure. Repeated sharing causes recipients to fork or comment on the live object; the bottleneck moves from rendering to provenance, access and merge rights. If the object cannot export faithfully or remain usable offline, people retreat to files/PDFs.
3. **Agentic abundance creates a review economy (new causal emphasis from candidate 7).** Research revealed products are moving directly toward agent-generated native files. When creating ten versions costs little, people may generate stakeholder-specific analyses rather than one universal deck. Recipients face comparison and accountability work; organizations may standardize evidence lineage, approval and “which version was acted on?” A new kind of demand emerges for traceable change sets and review workflows. If recipients ignore tailored output or AI output remains too unreliable, abundance instead triggers throttling and fewer artifacts.
4. **Workflow substrates detach from office brands (replacement path, sharpened from candidate 4/5).** Teams may keep a live dashboard, database, issue tracker, collaborative canvas or app as the operational source and generate an Office/PDF/HTML snapshot only when communicating or filing. For recurring decisions, nobody “writes a report” in the classic sense; agents query shared data, propose a decision, and attach a generated rationale to a workflow record. This is a different route from an AI agent editing Office files. Its hinge is whether institutions accept mutable, queryable records for accountability.
5. **Compatibility and policy make formats sticky (counter-path).** Even if most reading shifts to browser links, attachments to outside parties, records retention, macro-heavy spreadsheets, offline work and legacy archives can keep Office-compatible artifacts in circulation. Standards and interoperability reduce but do not erase this burden. The result may be dual demand: cheap public web delivery plus paid conversion, fidelity testing, offline work and controlled collaboration. This is not proof of indefinite incumbency; transition costs can decay as practices and contracts change.

## Candidate comparison without rankings

- Candidates 1 and 3 preserve a suite-like institution but can reduce app-opening frequency; candidate 2 moves the focal object from page to live source; candidate 4 removes document authorship from many recurring workflows; candidate 5 sustains file compatibility via external coordination costs.
- Remove product names: all but candidate 4 retain a portable artifact as a central object; candidate 4's object is a stateful workflow or dataset. This is the strongest causal difference worth developing.
- Near baseline: web office suites already co-edit and import/export office files; AI-assisted drafting already automates portions of document production. Added conditional mechanisms are the review burden of generated variants, live HTML views bound to a typed source, and decision workflows that generate records only at the point of institutional handoff. No claim of novelty is made.
