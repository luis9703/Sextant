# Reconciliation against actual review
Review file: 07-review.md. Scope: all six findings; no extra factual claim was added that would require a new source. The original public NBS source remains the only quantitative anchor; its boundaries are carried into report.

1. Networked family: **partially accept** (high). 06-pathways already named friends/partners, repeated small commitments, migration, resources, exit and mediation, but did not make initiation, labor rotation, substitute after rupture/cross-city move explicit. Report 1 now adds who initiates (participants), rotation and backup, distance/time/health constraints, and makes replacement/transfer uncertain. No assertion that these arrangements have legal force.
2. Services: **accept** (high). Report 3 separates routine paid tasks from acute night response/long-term disability care; makes payer, interruption and fallback person visible. No claim of current coverage or prices.
3. Neighborhood: **accept with narrowing** (medium). Report 2 now presents low-intensity information/help and acquaintance, not trust or crisis responsibility as automatic outcome; accessible hours, lease duration, language/disability access and cross-season continuity enter conditions/signposts.
4. Shared housing: **accept** (high). Report 4 explicitly separates chosen co-living from cost-driven compression; asks about privacy, safety, costs, exit burden and changing preference. A rise in shared residence is not treated as evidence of community intimacy.
5. Missing actors: **accept** (medium). Report 1/2/3/4 and cross-path signposts name remote kin, women care workers, tenants/migrants, disabled and low-income people, service workers/owners and people refusing networks. Report 5 leaves “enough connection” self-defined; support networks are not assumed free or gender-neutral.
6. Five paths coexist, no probability or linear convergence: **accept** (high). Report lead presents six overlapping possibilities as alternatives, not a sequence or national prediction. Census/WHO/OECD evidential limits are restated.
7. Public infrastructure low-obligation connection: **accept as a conditional additional path**, not a factual finding. Report 6 proposes public nodes plus formal referral as distinct from private mutual-aid and paid care; budget, non-digital access, accessibility, privacy, and crisis responsibility are necessary conditions. No basis to call it more likely.

Still unresolved: Chinese non-marital relationship/legal authorization and tenancy/care protections; actual local mutual aid and public service performance; cost and continuity of commercial services; population-level distribution and lived experience. New research or local fieldwork would be required. The central unresolved matter is not whether the forms are imaginable, but which people can rely on them and who absorbs care when they fail.
