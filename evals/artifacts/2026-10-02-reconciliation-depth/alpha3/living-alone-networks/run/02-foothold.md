# Foothold

- User observation: more acquaintances live alone and some prefer non-marriage with dependable companionship. No sampling frame, location, age profile, or comparison period was supplied; retain as a signal from the user's social circle.
- China NBS, Seventh National Population Census main results, 2021-05-11, https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202302/t20230203_1901080.html (accessed 2026-10-02). It reports 494.16 million family households and average household size of 2.62 in 2020, down from 3.10 in 2010; it attributes smaller household size partly to mobility and improved housing enabling newly married couples to live separately. It does not in this release give the count of single-person households, nor establish that non-marriage or desire for companionship caused the change.
- OECD Family Database SF1.1, https://www.oecd.org/els/family/database.htm and linked family-size composition notes (accessed 2026-10-02). Its definition distinguishes a single-person household from a multi-person household sharing resources; its cross-country categories are useful for measurement, not direct evidence for a Chinese trajectory. The linked PDF returned 404 during this run, so only the database page/search-indexed definition was available.
- Current evidence varies by age and support. A qualitative study of community-dwelling Chinese adults aged 80+ sampled in nine communities across Hangzhou/Shaoxing and a hospital (2023, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10481758/) describes home and relationships as belonging sources; sample is purposive and excludes people unable to interview or in institutions. - A Chinese cross-sectional study of older adults' willingness to join mutual aid (2025, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11919656/) reports associations with community activities, willingness both to give and accept help, neighbor relations, and not living alone. Cross-sectional association does not establish what causes participation or generalize to younger non-married adults. Full-page open was blocked by recaptcha; search result/index material only.
- Key ambiguity: living alone is a residence/household measure; solitude, loneliness, chosen kin, care availability, and marital status are separate variables. Cohousing is not equivalent to independent households plus a trusted support network.
